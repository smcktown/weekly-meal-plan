Tuesday · dinner

Beef & chicken liver udon

Same beef and liver as last week, in a broth instead of over rice. Thin sliced beef, the liver in small seared pieces through it. Soak 150 g of livers in milk for 20 minutes and pour the milk away, cut to about a centimetre, sear 60–90 seconds and take them straight out. Back into the grown-ups' bowls at the very end.

Why a broth this time: it is the best dish there is for two sittings an hour apart. The pot sits at a bare simmer and nothing gets worse — no reheated beef, no dried-out rice. The beef goes into the simmering broth for 60–90 seconds per bowl, the udon takes two minutes. The late sitting is as good as the early one.

The base, made once: kombu and bonito dashi (or a clean packet one), soy, mirin, a little maple, a slice of ginger. ⚠️ Check the dashi packet — instant powder is where onion and garlic hide.

Build it onion-free — infused oil, plenty of ginger, green tops only. Anyone who wants onion gets it wilted into their own bowl, not the pot.

Two veg: 🥬 bok choy cooked in the broth, two minutes — this is the one time boiling greens is fine, because everything that leaches out ends up in the soup you drink · red capsicum roasted and laid on top, plus a few cherry tomatoes on the side.

🔁 Buy about 1.4× the beef and bag 250 g of it raw and separate — in case Wednesday falls through the way it did last week. If it doesn't, that bag goes to the freezer untouched. Raw sliced beef keeps two days, and Wednesday is exactly two days out.

All the liver goes tonight — it reheats badly, so there's no second sitting for it.

Thursday · dinner

Soy-ginger braised chicken thigh with carrot

The little one's night — chicken, braised soft, mildly seasoned. Bone-in thigh if you can get it; the braise is better for it and it shreds easily.

Method: infused oil and a lot of ginger, brown the thighs skin-side down, then dashi or water with soy, mirin and a spoon of maple, carrots in chunks, lid on, 25–30 minutes. It sits on the stove and improves through both sittings — no reheating, no timing.

✅ Nobody's coming over this week, so the no-frying rule is off for once. The braise still wins the two sittings on its own merits — but it's worth knowing you had the choice.

Two veg: 🥬 spinach with sesame — blanch 30 seconds, squeeze it dry, dress with toasted sesame, soy and a little maple. Cold, made in the afternoon, out of the way · red capsicum raw in strips on the table, which keeps more of the vitamin C than roasting does.

Cook extra thighs. Shredded chicken makes Saturday lunch free, and Saturday morning is gone.
