Meal Plan · Week 10

Cook Tue + Thu + Fri · freezer-first · a different carb each night
🍳 Four cooks — freezer-first, and no rice every night. Cook Tue dinner, Thu (lunch + dinner), Fri dinner. Mon + Wed are eat-out. Three of four mains come from the freezer (chicken thighs, minced pork, minced beef) → the only protein to buy is prawns. Carbs rotate: sweet potato fries → bee hoon → pasta → rice. Tue + Thu are split-dinner nights — dishes hold warm (one sitting ~6pm, the rest ~7pm), and no fish those nights. Fri is the fish-safe night.
Heads up: almost everything's in the freezer already — just buy prawns, the fresh medley veg, sweet potatoes, and the noodles/pasta.
Tue · dinner holds warm 🍠 sweet potato fries
One-tray chicken + vegetable medley + sweet potato fries
⏱ 10 min hands-on · oven
Marinate in the AM. Chicken thighs (from the freezer) on a bed of capsicum, zucchini, cherry tomato & red onion; lined tray; oven 200 °C ~30–35 min. Sweet potato fries on their own lined tray — cut into batons, toss in oil + a light dusting of cornflour, 220 °C, flip halfway (own tray = they crisp instead of steaming). Two chicken trays for the split: tray 1 in ~5:35, tray 2 from the fridge ~6:35. Broil 2–3 min for char; re-crisp the fries under the broiler for the 7pm sitting. Little one: soft fries + chopped chicken. Low-carb one: extra chicken + double veg, skip the fries.
Thu · lunch family 🍜 bee hoon
Prawn & minced-pork bee hoon + egg
⏱ 15 min
Rice vermicelli tossed with prawns, minced pork (from the freezer), egg, spring-onion tops, a splash of soy & sesame. Quick and everyone eats it. Me: bigger prawn + pork portion; garlic-infused oil only. Little one: soft noodles, prawns chopped small.
Thu · dinner holds warm 🍝 pasta
Spaghetti bolognese (minced beef)
⏱ 15 min hands-on
Minced beef from the freezer → a proper slow-ish bolognese (soffritto, canned tomato, simmer). The sauce holds & reheats beautifully for the 6pm/7pm split — boil the pasta in two goes, or hold it cooked. Cook double → freeze half. Me: garlic-infused oil, and my sauce over rice vermicelli / a little GF pasta instead of wheat. Little one: soft mince, cut the pasta small.
Fri · dinner fish-safe night 🍚 rice
Teriyaki / miso salmon + rice + bok choy
⏱ 15 min · oven
Uses the salmon in the fridge. Marinate Thu night → oven 200 °C ~12 min; glaze burns fast, watch the last 3 min. The one no-split, no-rush night — and the one rice night, a nice change after the fries/noodles/pasta. Little one: flake, check for bones. Low-carb one: two fillets, skip rice. Freeze any extra salmon — travel week is coming.
