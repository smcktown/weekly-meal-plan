Tuesday · lunch

🥩 Beef & liver donburi — everyone at home

Prep after the morning workout, beef cooked when the kids walk in. Thin beef toughens if it's reheated, so the last step is four minutes at lunchtime.

Morning, half ten: ① Sauce base — garlic oil and ginger, one diced red capsicum, then dashi, soy, mirin and maple, ten minutes. ② Livers twenty minutes in milk, rinsed, patted very dry, cut small — seared sixty to ninety seconds, set aside. ③ Spinach namul — thirty seconds, squeezed, sesame. ④ Rice on the timer.

Lunchtime: sauce base back to a simmer, 400g sliced beef in, four minutes, spring onion greens. The liver goes into your bowl only — warm it in the sauce for the last minute. Kids get beef, rice, namul and capsicum.

🥤 Water for you, no coffee or tea for an hour either side. ⛔ No egg on your bowl.

Dinner: out, six to eight.

Monday · dinner · cook ×2

🥔 Pork & beef nikujaga, liver folded in

Uses all four leftovers, and Tuesday's dinner is made at the same time. Mince braises get better when reheated, potato and all.

About half four, 45 minutes: ① 200g chicken livers soaked twenty minutes in milk, rinsed, blitzed to a paste and stirred raw into the leftover pork + 500g lean beef mince. ② Garlic oil and grated ginger, then brown the mince. ③ All the leftover potatoes in big chunks and two carrots in thick coins. ④ 500ml dashi, three tablespoons each of soy and mirin, one of maple. Lid on, twenty minutes until the potato is soft. Spring onion greens.

Half goes into a box for Tuesday. On the table: rice, 🥬 spinach namul (thirty seconds, squeezed, sesame) and ⭐ raw red capsicum strips.

🥤 Water for me, a small orange juice for the kids. ⛔ No turmeric or curry powder in this one.
