Tuesday · cooked in the morning · lunch + dinner + Wednesday lunch

🥩 Tomato-braised beef with potato & carrot

Start at half ten, straight after the workout. About twenty minutes of hands-on work, then it simmers on its own until about half twelve. Mild, no chilli, soft enough for the little one, better reheated. One pot, three meals.

① Brown 1.2kg beef chuck chunks in two tablespoons garlic oil, in two batches, about four minutes each. ② Same pot: a thumb of ginger in slices, the green tops of four spring onions, one minute. Beef back in. ③ Add five chopped tomatoes, two tablespoons tomato paste, four tablespoons light soy, one of dark soy, one of maple, water just to cover. Lid on, lowest simmer, 1 hour 15. ⛔ No turmeric, no onion. ④ At quarter to twelve, four potatoes and three carrots in big chunks go in for the last 40 minutes.

⑤ While it simmers, my liver: the whole pack, patted dry, in 1cm pieces. Garlic oil, very hot pan, 60–90 seconds, out, still pink inside. Into a lidded box in the fridge. It never goes in the pot.

⑥ Also while it simmers, spinach namul, two bags: steamed thirty seconds, squeezed, sesame oil, salt, sesame seeds. Covers Tuesday and Wednesday.

⑦ Done: box a third of the pot for Wednesday lunch straight away.

My lunch, about 12:35: beef, potato and sauce over rice. A third of the liver into my bowl with boiling sauce ladled over, which is all the reheating it gets. Namul and raw capsicum strips. Kids, after pickup: the same, reheated.

Evening: reheat gently. Six for the girls, seven for the boys. Rice on keep-warm. 🥬 Bok choy ninety seconds per sitting, raw capsicum, namul. Liver: another third into my bowl, same way. The big kids can have two or three small pieces each. ⛔ None for the low-carb one, none for the little one.

🥤 Water for me, no coffee or tea an hour either side. Low-carb plate: no rice, skip the potato, extra beef and carrot, double bok choy.

Thursday · dinner · two sittings · the night before we leave

🍗 Rice-cooker chicken & ginger rice, spinach miso soup

Four o'clock, ten minutes. Then the rice cooker does the rest and keeps it warm for both sittings.

① Three cups rice, washed. Dashi to the three-cup line, take out five tablespoons, add three tablespoons soy and two of mirin. ② On top, don't stir: 800g boneless chicken thigh in bite-size pieces, two carrots in matchsticks, a thumb of ginger in fine strips. Normal cook. ③ Fold through once, spring onion greens.

Each sitting (six girls, seven boys): small pot of dashi, a spoon of miso stirred in off the boil, half a bag of spinach wilted for a minute. 🥬 Bok choy steamed ninety seconds. ⭐ Raw capsicum and the last cherry tomatoes on the plate.

The little one likes chicken, so this is her night. Low-carb plate: pick out extra chicken, half the rice, double bok choy and soup.

🧊 After dinner, empty the fridge. Anything left that won't keep a week goes out tonight.
