Tuesday · dinner

🥩 Beef & chicken-liver rice bowls

Components in separate bowls, everyone builds their own. This is the best answer there is to two sittings an hour apart — nothing is ever reheated, the late one is identical to the early one, and they take only what they'll eat.

The livers: 200 g soaked in milk 20 minutes, milk poured away, patted dry, trimmed, cut to about a centimetre. Sear 60–90 seconds and pull them straight out. ⚠️ Past two minutes they go chalky — that's the one thing that ruins this. They go into the grown-ups' bowls at the very end.

The beef: 600 g sliced thin, seared hot and fast in infused oil with ginger, soy, mirin, a little maple. Two batches, one per sitting — four minutes each, so seven o'clock gets beef cooked to order.

The toppings, all made in the afternoon, all served at room temperature: 🥬 spinach with sesame — blanched 30 seconds, squeezed dry, sesame oil and seeds · carrot, julienned, two minutes in a dry pan · ⭐ red capsicum raw in strips — raw keeps more of the vitamin C than cooking it · cucumber · a soft egg on top.

🌶 Chilli paste on the side, grown-ups only. The flavour goes in a dipping bowl, never into the pot.

🔁 Cook the beef at 1.5×. The extra becomes Thursday's lunch — a second iron meal for no second cook.

⏱ Tuesday is the heavy day, and it's the only one. Toppings and liver prep in the morning, about twenty-five minutes · fish in the oven at twenty to three · beef seared twice in the evening. Everything else this week is light.

Wednesday · lunch

🍙 Salmon-mayo onigiri + miso soup

Made in the morning, eaten cold or room-temp — and that's the point.

⚠️ Wednesday has a problem the other days don't: the eldest gets home about twenty to three and is at tuition at a quarter to. He cannot sit down to a cooked lunch. So this one is built to travel — two rice balls in a box, eaten in the car or there. The other two and you sit down at a quarter to three with the soup.

The filling: canned salmon with the bones mashed straight in — they break up completely and they're the calcium — Japanese mayo, a little lemon, black pepper. Rice made in the morning, cooled slightly, shaped around it, a strip of nori on each.

The soup: dashi, miso whisked in off the boil, tofu cubes, and 🥬 a big handful of baby spinach wilted in at the end.

🥬 The spinach is in the soup on purpose. It's the second leafy hit of the week — the one the Saturday salad used to be — and putting it in a broth you drink loses nothing, because what leaches out is still in the bowl.

Thursday · dinner

🍗 Poached chicken + rice cooked in the stock

✅ Nobody's coming over this week, so the no-frying rule is off — worth knowing, even though it doesn't change the answer. It's still a sport night, so the dish still has to survive two sittings, and a stir-fry doesn't.

This one holds better than anything else in the rotation. The chicken sits in its own warm stock and the rice is in the cooker — seven o'clock is genuinely as good as six. No reheating, no timing, nothing to watch.

The little one's night — poached chicken is the softest protein on the whole list.

Method: poach gently 30–35 minutes with ginger and the green tops of the spring onions, rest, slice. Cook the rice in the strained stock with a little infused oil. Ginger-scallion oil on the side — infused oil, grated ginger, green tops, hot oil poured over. Chilli sauce for the grown-ups only.

⚠️ Skim the stock before the rice goes in, and take the skin off your own portion. That's the difference between this being a good dish for you and a neutral one.

Two veg: 🥬 bok choy steamed, not boiled — two or three centimetres of water, lid on, two to three minutes, salt after · cucumber and tomato raw on the side, which is the traditional plate anyway.
