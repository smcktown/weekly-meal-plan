Tuesday · dinner

Beef & chicken liver donburi

Thin sliced beef, with the liver in small pieces through it. Soak 150 g of chicken livers in milk for 20 minutes and throw the milk away — that's what takes the metallic edge off. Cut them to about a centimetre, then sear hard and fast, 60–90 seconds, and take them straight out. ⚠️ Liver is the one thing here that punishes overcooking; past two minutes it turns chalky. Add them back to the bowls at the very end, 30 seconds to warm through.

⏱ Two parts, because this is not a twenty-minute braise. Thin slices toughen in a long simmer and toughen again on reheating. So: make the sauce base once — infused oil, ginger, two finely diced capsicum softened in it, then dashi, soy, mirin and a little maple, simmered 10 minutes. Keeps a week. Then cook the beef fresh in a ladle of it, four minutes, at each sitting. Every sitting gets beef cooked to order instead of beef that's been sitting, and it carries the next two dinners with no reheated beef anywhere.

Build it onion-free — infused oil, ginger and the green tops only; wilt onion into the other portions at the end if anyone wants it, not into the pot. ⚠️ Check the dashi packet — instant powder often carries onion and garlic.

The capsicum goes in, not alongside. Two cooked down into the base until they vanish; a third diced fresh and thrown in for the last minute of each sitting, so it stays bright and sweet and keeps most of its vitamin C.

Buy 2× — up from one-and-a-half, now that Wednesday needs feeding too. Portion 1 kg of sliced beef into three bags raw before you cook anything: ~450 g tonight · ~250 g Wednesday · ~300 g Thursday. Raw sliced beef keeps two days, so freeze Thursday's tonight and move it down Wednesday morning. 🔒 Bag it before you cook — once it's loose in the pan it disappears, and Thursday's is the one there's no slot left to replace.

All the liver goes tonight. It reheats badly, so there's none for Wednesday or Thursday. Tonight is the one.

Two veg: capsicum cooked through the beef · bok choy steamed, salted after — lid on, 2–3 cm of water, 2–3 minutes. Steam it, don't boil it.

Wednesday · dinner

Beef & capsicum donburi + a fried egg

🔄 Wednesday's plans fell through, so this turned into a cook — but not a new one. Tuesday's braise reheated over rice, one fried egg each on top, and whatever bok choy is left or a few slices of cucumber.

Ten minutes, nothing to buy, no second cook. The egg is what makes it read as a different dinner rather than Tuesday again.

Cook the beef fresh in the reserved sauce base — four minutes. Don't reheat Tuesday's; thin sliced beef reheated is the one thing that disappoints. The base is already made, so this really is a ten-minute dinner. No liver tonight — it all went yesterday.

Low-carb portion: skip the rice, two eggs, a bigger ladle, double the greens.

Thursday · dinner

Build-your-own wraps — crisp chicken legs & Tuesday's beef

The skin-on legs from the freezer. Move them to the fridge Wednesday morning to thaw. Air fryer, 200 °C, 25–30 min, skin side up. The skin renders and crisps itself — no oil in a pan, nothing to wipe off the floor.

⚠️ The beef is now cooked fresh — four minutes in a lidded pan of the reserved sauce base, rather than set out cold from Tuesday. A lidded simmer is not a fry: no splatter, nothing on the floor. This is the one cost of slicing the beef instead of mincing it — tonight gains a four-minute pan it didn't have.

The wrap is the carb — there is nothing else to cook. No potatoes, no rice, no second tray. Warm the tortillas in the basket for 30 seconds once the chicken is out.

Set it out and walk away: chicken pulled off the bone into a bowl, the braised beef alongside, lettuce, capsicum strips, tortillas. Self-serve at the early sitting and again at the late one — no reheating and no timing at all.

🌽 Corn tortillas, not flour. Warmed through they're soft enough that nobody notices, and they're the gentler option when you're eating two or three. One pack for the whole table.

Two veg: 🥬 lettuce salad · red capsicum raw in the wrap — raw keeps more of the vitamin C than roasting does.

Put a handful of baby spinach in the wraps too, alongside the lettuce. Raw, no cooking, and it disappears between the chicken and the sauce — but it's the second leafy hit of the week and that one matters.

Cook extra legs. Shredded chicken makes Saturday lunch free, and Saturday morning is gone.
