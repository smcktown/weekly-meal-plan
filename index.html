Meal Plan · Week 10

Cook Tue + Thu + Fri · beef · prawn · salmon
🍳 Four cooks, oven-led. Cook Tue dinner, Thu (lunch + dinner), Fri dinner. Mon + Wed are eat-out, no Tue lunch this week. Tue + Thu are split-dinner nights → dishes hold warm: one sitting ~6pm, the rest ~7pm, and no fish those nights. Fri is the fish-safe night. Proteins: chicken · prawn · beef · salmon.
Heads up: Tuesday clears the chicken thighs already in the freezer — buy only the fresh medley veg.
Tue · dinner oven · two trays holds warm
One-tray chicken + vegetable medley + rice
⏱ 10 min hands-on
Marinate in the AM. Chicken thighs (from the freezer) on a bed of capsicum, zucchini, cherry tomato & red onion; lined tray; oven 200 °C ~30–35 min — the chicken fat drips down and cooks the veg. Two trays for the split: tray 1 in ~5:35 for the first sitting, tray 2 from the fridge ~6:35 so it's fresh at 7. Broil 2–3 min at the end for the char. Little one: soft veg + chopped chicken. Low-carb one: extra chicken + double veg, skip rice.
Thu · lunch family
Prawn & egg fried rice + cucumber
⏱ 15 min
Day-old rice, prawns, egg, spring-onion tops, a splash of soy & sesame. Quick and everyone eats it. Me: bigger prawn portion + an extra egg. Little one: mild, prawns chopped small.
Thu · dinner oven · no-fry holds warm
Beef bulgogi tray + rice + broccoli
⏱ 10 min hands-on
Marinate in the AM (soy, pear/apple, sesame, spring-onion tops). Spread thin on a lined tray, oven 200 °C, broil the last few min. Holds warm beautifully for the 6pm/7pm split; no wok splatter. Blanch the broccoli alongside. Cook a little extra → grab-able next-day topper. Little one: a few soft strips + rice. Low-carb one: extra beef + double greens, skip rice.
Fri · dinner oven fish-safe night
Teriyaki / miso salmon + rice + bok choy
⏱ 15 min · oven
Uses the salmon in the fridge. Marinate Thu night → oven 200 °C ~12 min; glaze burns fast, watch the last 3 min. The one no-rush, no-split night. Little one: flake, check for bones. Low-carb one: two fillets, skip rice. Freeze any extra salmon — travel week is coming.
