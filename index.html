Meal Plan · Week 9

Cook Tue + Thu + Fri · salmon & chicken
🍣 Salmon & chicken week — all on the oven. Cook Tue (lunch + dinner), Thu (lunch + dinner), Fri dinner. Lunches are family meals — kids home midday; Fri lunch is a no-cook leftover off Thursday's double chicken. Tue + Thu are split-dinner nights → dishes hold warm: one sitting ~6pm, the rest ~7pm, and no fish on those nights. Fri is the fish-safe dinner. Proteins: salmon · chicken · pork.
Cross-meal flow: Thu oven tray chicken — cook double → Fri leftover chicken donburi (lunch)
Tue · lunch family
Teriyaki salmon rice bowls + soft egg + cucumber
⏱ 15 min · oven toaster
Salmon skin-on, oven toaster 200 °C ~10–12 min; brush teriyaki the last 3 min. Flake over rice with a soft egg, cucumber, sesame. Me: extra salmon + egg for protein. Little one: flake carefully, check for bones.
Tue · dinner oven · two trays holds warm
Ginger scallion chicken + rice + baby bok choy
⏱ 15 min hands-on
Marinate in the AM, split into two trays, leave tray 2 in the fridge. Tray 1 → oven ~5:35 for the first sitting; tray 2 → oven ~6:35, out fresh for the second at 7. Blanch bok choy between. Broil 2–3 min at the end for the char (that's what replaces the air fryer). Me: green scallion tops only + garlic-infused oil, no raw garlic/onion. Little one: pull out the ginger coins. Low-carb one: extra chicken, skip rice.
Thu · lunch family
Minced pork soboro don + soft egg + spinach
⏱ 15 min
Mince fried down with soy, mirin, sugar & ginger → over rice with a soft egg and blanched spinach. Kid-safe, no chilli. Me: bigger pork portion + extra egg. Little one: soft mince + rice + egg.
Thu · dinner oven · no-fry holds warm
One-tray chicken + vegetable medley + rice
⏱ 10 min hands-on
Marinate in the AM. Chicken thighs on a bed of carrot, zucchini, sweet potato & broccolini; lined tray; oven 200 °C ~30–35 min — the chicken fat drips down and cooks the veg. No splatter → kitchen stays clean for evening guests. Cook double → Fri lunch. Broil 2–3 min if pale. Little one: soft veg + chopped chicken. Low-carb one: extra chicken + double veg, skip rice.
Fri · lunch no cook family
Chicken donburi (off Thursday's double) + soft egg + greens
⏱ assemble only
Not a cook — slice Thursday's extra tray chicken over hot rice, add a soft egg from the batch, quick-wilt greens in garlic-infused oil. Feeds the kid who's home all day with zero effort. Me: bigger chicken + egg portion.
Fri · dinner oven fish-safe night
Miso salmon + rice + blanched greens
⏱ 15 min · oven
Marinate Thu night → oven 200 °C ~12 min. Miso burns fast — watch the last 3 min. The one no-soccer night, so fish is safe. Little one: flake, check for bones. Low-carb one: two fillets, skip rice.
