Tuesday · dinner · cooked in the morning

🍔 Stewed hambagu in tomato-dashi sauce, liver folded in

Half ten, straight after the workout, about forty-five minutes. Japanese hamburg steaks simmered in sauce — better reheated, soft for the little one, mild.

① 200g chicken livers, rinsed, patted dry, blitzed to a paste. ② Mix with 1kg lean beef mince, half a cup of rolled oats, two grated carrots, spring onion greens, a tablespoon each of grated ginger and soy, half a teaspoon of salt. ⛔ No egg — the oats bind it. Shape twelve patties, thumb dent in the middle. ③ Garlic oil, brown in two batches, two minutes a side. ④ Same pan: two cans chopped tomatoes, 250ml dashi, two tablespoons each of soy and mirin, one of maple, one diced red capsicum. Patties back in, lid on, fifteen minutes, turn once.

⑤ While it simmers — spinach namul: one bag, steamed thirty seconds, squeezed, sesame oil, salt, sesame seeds.

⑥ Box five patties with sauce for Thursday lunch before dinner touches the pot. Cool, fridge.

Evening: reheat with a splash of water. Six for the girls, seven for the boys. Rice on keep-warm. 🥬 Bok choy ninety seconds per sitting, raw capsicum strips, namul from the fridge.

🥤 Water for me. ⛔ No turmeric. Low-carb plate: no rice, extra patty and bok choy.

🫀 iron ★⭐ vitamin C🥬 spinach2 veg🍲 morning cook ×2

Thursday · dinner

🍗 Rice-cooker chicken & ginger rice

Quarter past four, ten minutes, then the rice cooker does it. Press start around half four before soccer — not the delay timer with raw chicken. Keep-warm holds it for both sittings.

Three cups rice, washed. Dashi to the three-cup line, take out five tablespoons, add three tablespoons soy and two of mirin. On top, don't stir: 800g chicken thigh in bite-size pieces, two carrots in matchsticks, a thumb of ginger in fine strips. Normal cook. Fold through once, spring onion greens.

Six girls, seven boys, straight from keep-warm. 🥬 Bok choy ninety seconds per sitting + cherry tomatoes.

Low-carb plate: small scoop, chicken picked from the top, two boiled eggs, double bok choy.
