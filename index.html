Meal Plan · Week 8

Cook Tue + Thu · family lunches
🍜 This week. Cook Tue (lunch + dinner), Thu (lunch + dinner), Fri lunch. Lunches are family meals — kids home midday. Tue + Thu are split-dinner nights → dishes hold warm: one sitting ~6pm, the rest ~7pm. No fish on those nights. Proteins: chicken · pork · tuna (+ tofu as an adult add-on — kids don't eat it).
Cross-meal flow: Thu oven pork — cook double → Fri leftover pork rice bowls
Tue · lunch family
Tuna-mayo rice bowls / onigiri + soft egg + cucumber
⏱ 10 min · no cook
Kewpie + canned tuna over rice (kids love it), soft-boiled egg, cucumber, nori. Me: extra tuna + egg for protein. Little one: soft tuna-rice, no big nori.
Tue · dinner defrost holds warm
Frozen doenjang soup (chicken) + rice + baby bok choy
⏱ 10 min · reheat
Freezer stash → defrost + reheat; rice cooker; blanch bok choy → soup holds warm for the ~6pm / ~7pm split. Me: eat the chicken + rice + veg, go easy on the broth (doenjang can bloat — FODMAP); add firm tofu for +protein. Little one: soft chicken + rice + soft greens. Low-carb one: extra chicken, skip rice.
Thu · lunch family
Macaroni soup with egg, ham & fishball
⏱ 15 min
Kids' favourite. Simmer light chicken / ikan-bilis stock, add macaroni, ham, fishballs, beaten egg, blanched greens. Me: swap macaroni → rice vermicelli (gut-safe) + extra egg/fishball; whey shake to top up. Little one: soft macaroni + chopped ham + egg.
Thu · dinner oven · no-fry holds warm
Miso-maple oven pork tenderloin (no-sear) + rice + blanched greens
⏱ 10 min hands-on
Oven, clean by design. Whisk miso + maple + soy + mirin; marinate tenderloin in the AM → lined tray → oven ~20–25 min, rest & slice. Rice cooker; blanch greens one pot. Cook double → Fri lunch. Little one: soft sliced pork + rice + greens. Low-carb one: extra pork, skip rice.
Fri · lunch family
Leftover pork rice bowls + soft egg + wilted spinach
⏱ 10 min
Slice Thu's extra pork over rice (kids love it), soft egg, quick-wilt spinach with garlic-infused oil. Me: bigger pork + egg portion. Little one: soft chopped pork + rice.
