Tuesday · cooked in the morning · lunch + dinner

🥩 Red-braised beef shin with daikon & carrot

Start at half ten, straight after the workout. About twenty minutes of hands-on work, then it simmers on its own until about half twelve. Mild, no chilli, soft enough for the little one, better reheated. One pot, three meals.

① Blanch 1.5kg beef shin chunks in boiling water for three minutes, then drain and rinse. ② Pot: two tablespoons garlic oil, a thumb of ginger in thick slices, the green tops of four spring onions. One minute. Beef back in. ③ Add three chopped tomatoes, four tablespoons light soy, two of dark soy, two of Shaoxing wine (none in the house? one of mirin), one of maple, two star anise, one cinnamon stick, water just to cover. Lid on, lowest simmer, 1 hour 45. ⛔ No turmeric, no five-spice. ④ At quarter to twelve, half a daikon and two carrots in big chunks go in.

⑤ While it simmers, my liver: the whole pack, patted dry, in 1cm pieces. Garlic oil, very hot pan, 60–90 seconds, out, still pink inside. Into a lidded box in the fridge. It never goes in the pot.

⑥ Also while it simmers, spinach namul, two bags: steamed thirty seconds, squeezed, sesame oil, salt, sesame seeds. Covers Tuesday and my Wednesday box.

⑦ Done: fish out the star anise and cinnamon. Box a third of the pot for Thursday lunch straight away.

My lunch, about 12:35: beef and daikon over rice. A third of the liver into my bowl with boiling sauce ladled over, which is all the reheating it gets. Namul and raw capsicum. Kids, after pickup: sandwiches, or rice with a ladle of the braise.

Evening: reheat gently. Six for the girls, seven for the boys. Rice on keep-warm. 🥬 Bok choy ninety seconds per sitting, raw capsicum, namul. Liver: another third into my bowl, same way. The big kids can have two or three small pieces each. ⛔ None for the low-carb one, none for the little one.

🥤 Water for me. Low-carb plate: no rice, extra beef and daikon, double bok choy.

🍗 Oven shoyu-ginger chicken, kabocha & capsicum

Oven only, no wok, so the floor stays clean. Four o'clock, five minutes of tossing, then thirty-five in the oven. Out before soccer.

🥩 Gyudon: simmered beef & enoki over rice

Start at half ten, straight after the workout. Done by quarter past eleven, well before I head out at half twelve. Mild, no chilli, soft enough for the little one, and it reheats well. One pan, two lunches.

① Rice cooker on, enough for two lunches. ② Wide pan: 500ml dashi, five tablespoons light soy, four of mirin, one of maple, a thumb of ginger in thin strips. Bring to a simmer. ③ Two packs of enoki, base cut off and pulled apart, in for three minutes. ④ 1.2kg thin-sliced beef in batches, loosened with chopsticks, two minutes per batch, just until no longer pink. Green tops of four spring onions in at the end. ⛔ No onion, no garlic: garlic oil only.

⑤ My liver, while the dashi heats: the whole pack, patted dry, in 1cm pieces. Garlic oil, very hot pan, 60–90 seconds, out, still pink inside. Into a lidded box. Never in the pan of beef.

⑥ Spinach goma-ae, two bags: steamed thirty seconds, squeezed hard, then two tablespoons toasted sesame (crushed), one of soy, one teaspoon maple. Covers Tuesday and Wednesday.

⑦ Done: box half the beef with its sauce for Wednesday straight away.

My lunch, about twelve: beef and enoki over rice, sauce spooned over. Half the liver into my bowl (no more than a palm, about 50–60g), with hot sauce ladled over it. Goma-ae and raw capsicum strips. Kids, after pickup: the same bowl, reheated two minutes. The big kids can have two or three small pieces of liver each. ⛔ None for the low-carb one, none for the little one.

🥤 Water for me, no coffee or tea an hour either side. Low-carb plate: no rice, extra beef, double goma-ae.

Dinner: bought. I'm out at eight.

Thursday · dinner · two sittings

🍗 Hainanese-style poached chicken & stock rice

Lunch: bought. Dinner starts at four, about fifteen minutes of hands-on work. Ready before the evening run-around.

① Big pot: 2 litres water, a thumb of ginger in thick slices, the green tops of four spring onions, two teaspoons salt. Boil. 1.2kg boneless chicken thighs, skin on, in. Lowest simmer, fifteen minutes. Heat off, lid on, and leave it.

② Rice: three cups, washed, fried two minutes in two tablespoons garlic oil with grated ginger. Into the rice cooker with the chicken stock to the line instead of water, and a knotted pandan leaf if there is one. Normal cook.

③ Ginger sauce: three tablespoons grated ginger, chopped spring onion greens, half a teaspoon salt, four tablespoons hot oil poured over.

Each sitting (six girls, seven boys): lift out the chicken and slice it. Ladle stock into a small pot, half a bag of spinach wilted in for a minute. That's the soup. 🥬 Bok choy blanched ninety seconds. ⭐ Cucumber, cherry tomatoes and raw capsicum on the plate. The chicken waits in the warm stock between sittings.

The little one likes chicken, so this is her night. Low-carb plate: extra chicken, no rice, double bok choy and soup.
