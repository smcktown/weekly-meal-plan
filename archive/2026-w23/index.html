Tuesday · cooked late morning · lunch + dinner

🍔 Stewed hambagu in tomato-dashi sauce, liver folded in

Eleven o'clock, as the cleaner leaves, about forty-five minutes. Japanese hamburg steaks simmered in sauce — better reheated, soft for the little one, mild. One pot, three meals: my lunch today, everyone's dinner, Thursday lunch. ⚠️ Liver in the ovals only — never the low-carb one's. Big oval = mine · small oval = the two big kids · round = no liver (him and the little one).

① the whole pack of chicken livers (150–200g, whatever it is), rinsed, patted dry, blitzed to a paste, kept in its own bowl. No weighing. ② In a big bowl, 1.2kg lean beef mince with two-thirds of a cup of rolled oats, two grated carrots, spring onion greens, a tablespoon each of grated ginger and soy, half a teaspoon of salt. ⛔ No egg — the oats bind it. Split it: about 600g of the mix (a bit under half) into the liver bowl → six BIG OVALS, mine, and four SMALL OVALS, half the size, for the two big kids. The rest → ten round patties, no liver. Thumb dent in the middle. ③ Garlic oil, brown in batches, two minutes a side. ④ Same pan: two cans chopped tomatoes, 300ml dashi, two tablespoons each of soy and mirin, one of maple, one diced red capsicum. Patties back in, lid on, fifteen minutes, turn once.

⑤ While it simmers — spinach namul: one bag, steamed thirty seconds, squeezed, sesame oil, salt, sesame seeds.

⑥ Box Thursday lunch straight away: two big ovals (mine), two small ovals and four round, with sauce.

My lunch, quarter past twelve: two oval patties, rice, namul, raw capsicum. Then cool the pot and fridge it. Kids, after pickup: ham and cheese sandwiches they make themselves — there's half an hour before gym.

Evening: reheat with a splash of water. Six for the girls, seven for the boys. Me: two big ovals. Big kids: one small oval + one round each. Little one: a round, cut small. Him: rounds only. Rice on keep-warm. 🥬 Bok choy ninety seconds per sitting, raw capsicum strips, namul from the fridge.

🥤 Water for me. ⛔ No turmeric. Low-carb plate: no rice, extra patty and bok choy.

Tuesday · cooked in the morning · lunch + dinner

🥩 Red-braised beef shin with daikon & carrot

Start at half ten, straight after the workout. About twenty minutes of hands-on work, then it simmers on its own until about half twelve. Mild, no chilli, soft enough for the little one, better reheated. One pot, three meals.

① Blanch 1.5kg beef shin chunks in boiling water for three minutes, then drain and rinse. ② Pot: two tablespoons garlic oil, a thumb of ginger in thick slices, the green tops of four spring onions. One minute. Beef back in. ③ Add three chopped tomatoes, four tablespoons light soy, two of dark soy, two of Shaoxing wine (none in the house? one of mirin), one of maple, two star anise, one cinnamon stick, water just to cover. Lid on, lowest simmer, 1 hour 45. ⛔ No turmeric, no five-spice. ④ At quarter to twelve, half a daikon and two carrots in big chunks go in.

⑤ While it simmers, my liver: the whole pack, patted dry, in 1cm pieces. Garlic oil, very hot pan, 60–90 seconds, out, still pink inside. Into a lidded box in the fridge. It never goes in the pot.

⑥ Also while it simmers, spinach namul, two bags: steamed thirty seconds, squeezed, sesame oil, salt, sesame seeds. Covers Tuesday and my Wednesday box.

⑦ Done: fish out the star anise and cinnamon. Box a third of the pot for Thursday lunch straight away.

My lunch, about 12:35: beef and daikon over rice. A third of the liver into my bowl with boiling sauce ladled over, which is all the reheating it gets. Namul and raw capsicum. Kids, after pickup: sandwiches, or rice with a ladle of the braise.

Evening: reheat gently. Six for the girls, seven for the boys. Rice on keep-warm. 🥬 Bok choy ninety seconds per sitting, raw capsicum, namul. Liver: another third into my bowl, same way. The big kids can have two or three small pieces each. ⛔ None for the low-carb one, none for the little one.

🥤 Water for me. Low-carb plate: no rice, extra beef and daikon, double bok choy.

🍗 Oven shoyu-ginger chicken, kabocha & capsicum

Oven only, no wok, so the floor stays clean. Four o'clock, five minutes of tossing, then thirty-five in the oven. Out before soccer.

Big bowl: 1kg boneless chicken thighs, three tablespoons soy, two of mirin, one each of maple, grated ginger and garlic oil. Tray: half a kabocha in wedges, skin on, and two red capsicums in strips, olive oil and salt. Chicken on top. 200°C, thirty-five minutes, then leave it in the switched-off oven.

Six girls, straight from the oven. Seven boys, back in at 180°C for eight minutes. Rice on keep-warm. 🥬 Bok choy ninety seconds per sitting. Tray soaking by quarter to eight.

Low-carb plate: extra thigh, kabocha instead of rice, double bok choy.
