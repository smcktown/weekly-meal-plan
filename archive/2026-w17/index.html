Tuesday · dinner

🍝 Spaghetti bolognese, with chicken liver folded in

Sport at five, so it's two sittings again — six and seven. This is the easiest dish yet for that: the pot just sits there and gets better. No per-bowl work at all.

The liver goes in blitzed, not in pieces this time — and that's the right call here. Blended into a ragù it vanishes completely; it's the classic way it's done. Seared pieces go chalky in a long simmer, blitzed liver doesn't. Nobody picks anything out and nobody knows.

Doubling the pot is what pays back the dilution. Spread through a big sauce your share gets smaller — but you eat from it twice, tonight and Thursday lunch, which puts you back where a concentrated portion would have. And it keeps you comfortably inside the safe ceiling both times, with no portioning to think about.

The livers first: 250 g soaked in milk for 20 minutes, milk poured away, patted dry, pale stringy bits trimmed. Blitz them raw with a stick blender and a splash of the tomato until smooth.

Then the sauce: infused oil · 2 grated carrots and 1 grated red capsicum softened 6–8 minutes — that's the base, the vegetables and the vitamin C all at once · 1 kg lean mince browned properly, don't rush it · the liver paste in, cooked out 3–4 minutes until it loses its raw colour and disappears · tomato paste fried out a minute · two tins of plain chopped tomatoes · bay, oregano, a sprig of rosemary · simmer 45–50 minutes, lid ajar · a splash of milk at the end, salt last.

⚠️ Read the tomato label. Bottled pasta sauces almost always hide onion and garlic — plain passata or plain chopped tomatoes only.

⚠️ Lean mince, not fatty. The beef stays for the iron; the cut is what changes.

🌾 Brown rice spaghetti for the whole table — one product, nothing separate to manage, same call as the corn tortillas. Cook it per sitting, not all at once — rice pasta goes gluey sitting in a colander, and two lots of eight minutes is what the split wants anyway.

Two veg: carrot and capsicum are cooked in · 🥬 a big handful of baby spinach wilted into the pot for the last two minutes · cherry tomatoes raw on the table. Parmesan over.

Two rules, and they replace everything else.

① 🥚 The filling half is negotiable. The protein half is not. One from the second table, every morning.

② 🍬 Sweet fillings — two mornings a week between them. Everything else is free choice, including the same thing twice.
🫙 Oat jars — batch four, Sunday and Wednesday. Six minutes, keeps three or four days, and then it's a grab item like everything else.

Per jar: ½ cup rolled oats · ½ cup milk · 2 tbsp yogurt · pinch of salt (a teaspoon of chia thickens it).

🥇 Yours takes a scoop of powder — that's about 30 g of protein before eight in the morning, and breakfast is the meal where your day usually leaks. It's the single highest-value thing on this page.

🍓 Toppings in the morning, never into the jar.
🥚 Twelve eggs, Saturday night, peeled. Not Sunday — Sunday goes church, sport, out. Top up Wednesday morning.
⛔ Nothing here needs a pan at 6:45am. Scrambled eggs, fresh pancakes and prata go to the weekend. Night-before oven work is a different thing, and welcome.
