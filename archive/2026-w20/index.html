Tuesday · lunch

🥩 Beef & liver donburi — everyone at home

Prep after the morning workout, beef cooked when the kids walk in. Thin beef toughens if it's reheated, so the last step is four minutes at lunchtime.

Morning, half ten: ① Sauce base — garlic oil and ginger, one diced red capsicum, then dashi, soy, mirin and maple, ten minutes. ② Livers twenty minutes in milk, rinsed, patted very dry, cut small — seared sixty to ninety seconds, set aside. ③ Spinach namul — thirty seconds, squeezed, sesame. ④ Rice on the timer.

Lunchtime: sauce base back to a simmer, 400g sliced beef in, four minutes, spring onion greens. The liver goes into your bowl only — warm it in the sauce for the last minute. Kids get beef, rice, namul and capsicum.

🥤 Water for you, no coffee or tea for an hour either side. ⛔ No egg on your bowl.

Dinner: out, six to eight.
