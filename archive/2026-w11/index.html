Ajitsuke tamago is the trick worth learning. Soft-boil 6 minutes, ice bath, peel, then sit overnight in equal parts soy, mirin and water. Keeps 3–4 days. Same egg they already accept, completely different taste — that's how the rotation gets variety without fighting anyone on texture.
Sunday night: boil a dozen. Six get marinated for Wednesday; the rest cover Tuesday, Thursday and the snack boxes.
